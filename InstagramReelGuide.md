## 1. Reel Specifications & Best Practices

* **Duration:** 45 - 60 seconds (Optimal for Instagram algorithms)
* **Format & Aspect Ratio:** Vertical 9:16 (1080 x 1920 pixels)
* **Pacing:** Fast, energetic, visually stimulating with clean screen captures
* **Audio Track:** Upbeat, trending tech/lo-fi electronic or motivational beat (lowered to 15-20% under voiceover)
* **Tools for Recording & Editing:**
  * **Screen Recording:** OBS Studio (canvas set to 1080x1920) or Screen Studio / Loom
  * **Mobile / Web Preview:** Open `index.html` in Chrome/Edge (use Developer Tools Responsive Mode 1080x1920 or full screen)
  * **Video Editor:** CapCut, Premiere Pro, or DaVinci Resolve (auto-captions enabled)
